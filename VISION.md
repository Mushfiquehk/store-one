1. Store-one is a POS system that allows a new business to setup and operate their point-of-sale system for free in a way that helps boost their business into getting a second location. Our guiding star is to create the best foundation and easiest path to grow a business.
2. Store-one is an AI Agent first POS. Along with an intuitive UI, the operational load can be completely driven by an AI agent living alongside the system. The agent can perform all tasks required to setup and maintain a locations's POS system like creating products, combos, discounts, promotions etc. A restaurant operator does not have to become a full time system administrator to maintain the Store-One POS.
3. The platform provides all software services required to operate the business including inventory, payroll, accounting, and marketing all as optional add-on features that the operator can setup and pay for later. The operator can optionally integrate 3rd party vendors to manage those aspects of their business.
4. The internal product (sales item) model must be flexible enough to allow creating a 1/2 & 1/2 CYO Pizza with unlimited toppings. The topping amounts in the recipe will be dynamically calculated based on the total topping amounts, using a matrix setup by the operator. The depletion of the amount of recipe ingredients of the items are the most accurate in the industry. This is imperative to accurate expense calculations and forecasts in COGS.
5. The store operator can view profitability over any period of time which Store-One calculates by tracking all revenues and expenses. The platform offers AI overviews of what affected profitability during that period and a few recommendations to improve.
6. Store-one has an autopilot mode where an AI agent takes over and takes the recommended actions and day to day operations like scheduling, payrolling and purchasing ingredients. With enough logging, it produces autopsy's of it's decisions taken in the past and even taken by the operator to self-improve it's decision making capabilities. 
7. Store-one's onboarding is streamlined for quick adoption. Once setup, and more locations are added to the business, the cloud enterprise experience becomes the main dashboard for executive overviews.
8. The built-in AI agent has a chat interface that can drive business actions on an enterprise and individual location level. Long projects like marketing promotions can be triggered and observed and autopsied on sessions running for long periods of time.
9. The zero cost setup and operation for a single location enticing for new restaurants aiming to grow their business or even just create a single hyper successful restaurant. The AI capabilities are BYOK and can be used on individual locations as well. All additional features beyond the POS core functionality are add-on services.
